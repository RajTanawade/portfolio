UX Case Study · Sports Rental & Club Services · Mobile App

Reneq — Access sports equipment & club services on demand.

RENEQ is a dedicated platform for sports enthusiasts. Easily borrow rental sports equipment delivered directly to your home, and enjoy hassle-free booking of club services for sports events.

01 · Overview & Context

Why Reneq? A change in everyday life & the access gap.

Due to busy city lives, people often neglect the physical activity their bodies need. Work-from-home settings saved commute hours that could be invested in playing sports with family and friends at home.

However, not everyone owns sports equipment for casual play. Buying an entire kit for occasional use feels wasteful, creating the need for a dedicated rental platform.

Why Reneq? People who casually feel the need for borrowing rented sports equipment need a platform that can serve the purpose.

02 · The Problem

Untimely sports urges vs. lack of equipment.

Sports enthusiasts find it difficult to fulfill a sudden urge to play when they have no equipment at home. Searching online for far-away local stores without delivery options often leads to annoyance—and dropping plans to stick back to screens all day.

Target User

Adults aged 23–35 with a sedentary lifestyle who feel the urge to indulge in sports during free time.

Core Needs

Borrow equipment on rent for occasional play without full ownership; easily book sports event services.

Key Insights

Club membership benefits avoid idle equipment costs while serving workplace or residential society events.

04 · User Research

Uncovering real user needs.

User research was conducted through structured online surveys focused on frequency of play, equipment availability, rental duration expectations, and interest in club services.

90.9%

Preferred a mobile app over a desktop website

72.7%

Felt the need to learn a new sport or excel in a known sport

63.6%

Would borrow rental equipment when gathering with friends in free time

63.6%

Struggled to find professional organizers for office/society sports events

Casual Access Matters

Users prefer rental as a practical alternative to buying an expensive complete sports kit for occasional use.

Learning is Essential

Opportunity extends beyond hardware: users want coaching, scheduled batches, and skill improvement.

Events are Underserved

Significant demand to connect club infrastructure and professional referees with corporate or society tournaments.

05 · Competitor Analysis

Evaluating fragmented solutions.

Analyzing existing platforms revealed gaps between general rental services and specialized sports club management.

06 · User Persona

Amit Singh — The Sports Enthusiast

A software engineer seeking active leisure time without the burden of equipment ownership.

SPORTS ENTHUSIAST

Amit Singh

Age 28 · Software Developer · Pune, India

Feels trapped by long sedentary working hours. Eager to play badminton with friends and family during weekends, but buying a full kit feels wasteful for occasional play.

"I want to find rental sports equipment online & get it home-delivered whenever needed."

Daily Routine

Work long hours at desk → Rush home drained → Spend free time on screens → Miss playing sports.

Goals & Needs

Play badminton regularly; rent quality equipment on demand; spend active time with family.

Frustrations

Sedentary weight gain; dislike traveling far to local shops after work; high cost of buying full kits.

Ideal Solution

Mobile app to select sports equipment, specify rental duration, and receive home delivery within hours.

07 · Customer Journey Map

From frustration to a successful rental.

Tracing Amit's experience across 7 key stages of interaction.

StageUser ActionThoughtsEmotionUX Opportunity
1. Awareness Feels sudden urge to play; local shops require buying full kit. "Sedentary life is making me unfit... I need equipment." Frustrated Highlight rental convenience across social media channels.
2. Discovery Searches online for equipment rental services. "Where can I find rental equipment with delivery?" Hopeful Provide clear onboarding value propositions.
3. Onboarding Downloads Reneq, signs up via phone/Google. "Is this app easy to use?" Expectant Allow catalog exploration before requiring membership.
4. Membership Explores 3, 6, 12 month membership perks & discounts. "Can I get coaching and club access too?" Interested Transparently list membership perks & trial benefits.
5. Rental Selects Badminton → Rackets & shuttlecocks → Duration. "How simple is the delivery & checkout?" Confident Streamlined multi-step form with upfront price breakdowns.
6. Delivery & Use Receives home delivery and plays with family. "I did it! Borrowing equipment is hassle-free." Ecstatic Real-time delivery tracking & post-play reviews.
7. Return / Renewal Gets expiry notification; extends rental or schedules pickup. "So easy! Reneq saved my weekend." Satisfied 1-click rental extension or scheduled return pickup.

09 · User Flow Architecture

Full swimlane user flow.

Interlinked swimlanes map the end-to-end paths across five core domains: Login, Membership, Instructor, Rental Equipment, and Club Services.

Equipment Rental Path

Club Services → Borrow Rental Equipment → Choose Sport → Select Quantity & Days → Address → Bill & Payment → Home Delivery → Extension / Return.

Club & Event Services Path

Club Services → Book Services for Events → Event Scope & Dates → Check Location Availability → Referees & Equipment → Billing Quote → Payment Confirmation.

12 · High-Fidelity UI

The final product experience.

The final interface translates the product proposition into dedicated experiences for onboarding, membership, members, instructors, equipment rental and sports-event services.

High Fidelity Onboarding Screens (assets/hifi.png) Onboarding and membership screens
Original case-study visual — high-fidelity onboarding and membership screens.

Onboarding

Users scroll through splash screens that introduce the Training, Renting Equipment and Club Booking features of the app.

  • Sign up easily with Gmail or phone number.
  • Reset password and activate the account.
  • Create a minimal member profile.
  • Reach the main experience without unnecessary friction.
Member Homepage Screens (assets/member.png) Member homepage and navigation
Original case-study visual — member homepage and navigation.

Member screens

The homepage is designed to comprise charts at a glance that update daily. Users can navigate through different tabs using the Navigation Bar, and CTA buttons on the homepage and other pages drive user action.

Instructor & Member Screen Exploration (assets/instructor.png) Instructor screens & batch planning
Original case-study visual — instructor and member screen exploration.

Instructor screens

Instructors work as a bridge between the club and members, so the case study treats their screens as a distinct experience.

  • Write rules and regulations.
  • Plan batches.
  • Look after club inventory.
  • Keep tabs on member performance.
Rental Equipment Flow (assets/rental.png) Borrowing rental equipment from the club
Original case-study visual — borrowing rental equipment from the club.

Borrowing rental equipment from the club

  1. Tap the Club Services icon from the Navigation Bar.
  2. Tap Borrow Rental Equipments.
  3. Select the choice of sport and get a list of equipment.
  4. Choose the equipment, quantity and rental duration.
  5. Enter the delivery address.
  6. Tap Deliver to this Address to receive home delivery.
  7. Review the detailed bill.
  8. Tap Pay to open the payment gateway.
  9. Complete payment and receive the home delivery.
Sports Event Booking Flow (assets/event.png) Booking the club and its services for sports events
Original case-study visual — booking the club and its services for sports events.

Booking the club & its services for sports events

The experience is designed as a comprehensive page for organising a sports event.

  • Tap Club Services → Book Club Services for Events.
  • Check location availability for events at an office or society.
  • Check date availability to avoid untimely cancellations and book in advance.
  • The address page only triggers if the member does not select “Book the Club for the event period”; otherwise it is automatically bypassed.
  • Review billing information with a total quote for organising the event.
  • Review the amount and checkout through the payment gateway.
